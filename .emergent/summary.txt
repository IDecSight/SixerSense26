<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a mobile-first, AI-powered gamified productivity app called **Sixer Sense**.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (Summary of all user requests):**

*   **Vision:** Turn daily productivity into a cricket match. Tasks are balls, completing them scores runs, and distractions cause wickets.
*   **Core Gameplay:**
    *   **Match:** One match per day, max 11 tasks (Playing XI). 6 tasks = 1 over.
    *   **Task Categories:** Health, Career, Finance, Relationships, Spiritual, Travel.
*   **User Tiers & Monetization:**
    *   **Free:** Basic gameplay.
    *   **Premium:** Unlocks advanced features via a subscription (₹99/monthly, ₹699/yearly) handled by Razorpay.
    *   **Premium Features:** Google Calendar sync, automated health tracking (Google Fit), advanced AI commentary, and an Insights section.
*   **Core Features:**
    *   **Authentication:** Google Sign-In and Email/Password.
    *   **Goal Setting:** A natural language prompt on the Home Page allows users to set short, medium, and long-term goals. The AI parses these goals, updates the user's profile, and generates a Playing XI (task list) for the day.
    *   **Today's Match (Dashboard):** Main gameplay screen showing the Scoreboard, Captain's Call (AI recommendations), the Playing XI (task list), and Match Summary.
    *   **Analytics:**
        *   **Cricket Ground:** A visual representation of productivity across the 6 life categories.
        *   **Insights Tab:** Analytics on focus distribution, over/underplayed life areas, and AI-driven plans.
    *   **Profile:** Extended to include playing style and auto-calculated strongest/weakest life categories.
*   **AI Captain:** Provides cricket-style commentary, recommends tasks, and helps with goal setting.

**User's preferred language**: English
